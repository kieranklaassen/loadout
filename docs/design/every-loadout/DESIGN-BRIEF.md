# Every Loadout design brief (shared)

Product: "Every Loadout" (every.to/loadout). Every staff and Every subscribers rank their top three AI tools per kind of work (11 kinds: Coding, Knowledge work, Writing, Research, Classification, Image, Video, Animation, Text to speech, Speech to text, Music). A pick = TOOL (the app, e.g. Claude Code, Cursor) + MODEL (the AI behind it, e.g. Claude Opus 5.5) + optional CONTEXT (200K, 1M) + EFFORT (low/medium/high). The home page shows what the team uses; profiles are private by default; agents (Claude, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex) can fill loadouts over MCP, their picks stay "suggested" until the person confirms. Model launches link to Every's Vibe Checks (every.to/vibe-check/...) and takes link to checks.every.to.
Hard product rules: no engagement mechanics (no streaks, leaderboards of PEOPLE, nudges); show counts like "5 of 6 use it", never points, medals or squares on simple pages; private by default.

Design language (Every dark): page #020202 with a faint dot grid, cards #111111 with 1px #2a2a2a, text #fdfaf7, secondary #d0d0d0, muted #8c8d91 (never dimmer), sky blue #9ce5f5 ONLY for primary CTA, active filter and the italic brand word, yellow #f6b90f (from the Every collage art) for NEWEST/rank-1 accents only, coral #ff7765 only for stale/danger. Headings in Newsreader serif (class "d"), UI in Hanken Grotesk, captions in Geist Mono (class "mono"). Sharp corners (2-4px). TOOLS are square light tiles (class "tile"), MODELS are round light tiles. Section spacing 72-80px. Min text size: 12px for uppercase mono labels, 13px for anything else. Body copy in plain language, sentence case, no jargon.
