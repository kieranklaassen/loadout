Loadout supports WebMCP. While you are signed in, a browser agent that supports it can use the same tools on this site. There is nothing to install or connect, and the same limits apply: its picks stay suggestions until you confirm them.

What it can and can't do

  • Its picks show as suggestions until you confirm them.
  • It can read your loadout and the team's rankings.
  • It can add or change picks, shown as suggestions until you confirm them.
  • It can't change your visibility or delete your account.
